# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive,
assume good intent while examining evidence, and critique work without attacking
people. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately,
and protect reporter confidentiality where possible.

This policy is intentionally short for a small project. It is inspired by the
Contributor Covenant and may be replaced by the full covenant as the community
grows.
